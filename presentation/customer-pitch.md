## Page 2 — Core Operations Management

This is the heart of the system — the day-to-day engine that runs your fleet.

- **Trip / Running Chart** — Plan, assign, and track every trip from start to finish.
- **Driver–Cleaner Pairing** — Manage crew assignments per vehicle and trip.
- **Trip Expense Entry** — Capture toll, parking, and miscellaneous trip costs as they happen.
- **Fuel Entry** — Log fuel purchases per vehicle, with consumption tracked automatically.
- **Vehicle Expense Entry** — Record repairs, maintenance, and servicing costs against each vehicle.
- **General Expense Entry** — Track operational costs that don't tie to a specific vehicle or trip.
- **Driver Advance, Loan & Deduction Management** — Handle staff advances, loans, and deductions cleanly, with full repayment tracking.
- **Payment / Salary Disbursement** — Run payroll for drivers and cleaners directly from operational data.
- **Driver & Cleaner Salary Settlement** — Automatically reconcile earnings, advances, loans, and deductions into a final settlement.
- **Cash Flow Tracking** — See money in and money out across the entire operation in real time.

**The result:** every trip, every rupee spent, and every staff payment is logged, traceable, and tied back to a specific vehicle, driver, or job — eliminating leakage and guesswork.

## Page 3 — Master Data & Company Management

A strong operation starts with clean, centralized records. These modules are the foundation everything else is built on.

**Master Files**
- Driver Master File — full driver profiles, licenses, and history
- Vehicle Master File — registration, specifications, and lifecycle data
- Cleaner Master File — crew records and assignment history
- Customer Master File — client details, contacts, and billing info
- Item Master File — goods/cargo types handled in your operations
- Location Master File — routes, depots, and delivery points
- General Expense Type — standardized expense categories for clean reporting

**Company Management**
- Company Profile — your business identity, settings, and configuration
- User Creation & Management — onboard staff with controlled access
- User Permissions — role-based access so each team member only sees what they need
- Password Management — secure, self-service password changes
- Edit / Deactivate User — manage staff lifecycle as your team changes
- Activity Log — a full audit trail of who did what, and when

**Why it matters:** centralized master data means no duplicate entries, no inconsistent records, and no time wasted re-entering the same information across departments.
